# Pricing Policy

This document defines the pricing rules enforced across all order types.

## Discount Limits

- Standard orders: maximum 30% discount
- Bulk orders (>100 units): maximum 30% discount (no exception without manager approval)
- Subscription orders: maximum 20% discount on renewal

## Approval Thresholds

- Discounts above 30%: require manager approval
- Orders above $10,000: require manager approval regardless of discount

## Audit Requirements

All discount overrides must be logged with: user ID, order ID, requested discount, approved discount, approver ID.
